Explore the relationship between blood glucose levels and nutritional components for 16 subjects. Interact with the visualization below to gain insights.

Our Data Foundation

We built this platform using the BIG IDEAs Lab Glycemic Variability and Wearable Device Data from PhysioNet, capturing dietary logs and glucose responses from 16 participants. Our team cleaned and processed the dataset, combining food consumption within an hour into single meals and filtering meals at least 4 hours apart to account for glucose response delays.

What Our Visualizations Reveal

Our interactive visualizations uncover the nuanced relationship between nutrient intake and blood glucose responses. Here’s what each component reveals:

Time Histogram

X-Axis: 0–24 hours, representing the time of day.
Y-Axis: Number of meal events per hour.
Insight: Discover when meals are most frequently consumed, revealing eating patterns across the day.

Nutrient Histograms

X-Axis: Nutrient intake (e.g., carbs, protein, sugar) grouped into bins (e.g., 10g for carbs).
Y-Axis: Number of meal events in each nutrient range.
Insight: Understand the distribution of nutrient consumption, highlighting common intake levels.

Scatter Plot

X-Axis: Nutrient selected (sugar or carbohydrate, via dropdown).
Y-Axis: Glucose increase (mg/dL) within 2 hours post-meal.
Point Size: Proportional to fat content.
Insight: Visualize how nutrients correlate with glucose spikes, with tooltips showing meal details.

Interacting with Our Visualizations

Our platform is designed for intuitive exploration. Here’s how you can dive in:

  • Filter by Participant: Use the dropdown to focus on a participant’s data.
  • Toggle Nutrients: Switch between sugar and carbohydrate in the scatter plot.
  • Brush to Highlight: Brush the scatter plot to highlight nutrient ranges in histograms, or brush histograms to filter the scatter plot.
  • Hover for Details: Hover over scatter plot points to view nutrient data in tooltips.
  • Reset Filters: Clear selections with the “Reset” button.

Pro Tip: Try brushing the sugar and time histograms simultaneously to find high-sugar, late-night meals and see their glucose impact in the scatter plot!

About This Study

This study explores the intricate relationship between dietary nutrients and blood glucose levels across 16 subjects. By leveraging interactive visualizations, we aim to uncover patterns and correlations that inform better dietary choices for managing glycemic variability. Our team of dedicated researchers and developers has collaborated to create this platform, combining expertise in data science, visualization, and health informatics.

Technical Highlights

Our design decisions balance clarity, interactivity, and insight:

  • Unified Visualization Layout: We merged sugar and carbohydrate scatter plots into one with a nutrient toggle, reducing cognitive load while enabling comparison.
  • Bidirectional Brushing: Brushing scatter plots highlights histogram bars, and vice versa, empowering exploration of both aggregate trends and individual meals.
  • Tooltip Design: Scatter plot tooltips display comprehensive nutrient data, keeping the interface uncluttered.
  • Dynamic Scaling: Axes and point sizes adjust dynamically for readability across nutrient selections and filters.
  • Alternatives Considered: We explored line plots for glucose trends but chose scatter plots for granular meal comparisons. Bar charts were replaced with histograms for smoother binning.

These choices reflect our commitment to a robust, user-centric platform that balances precision and accessibility.
